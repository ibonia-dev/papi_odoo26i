Papi Payment Provider for Odoo

Accept MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money and card payments in Madagascar

A native Odoo payment provider

Papi appears next to the other payment providers of Odoo. Your customers select Papi at checkout, pay on the secure Papi page with the instrument of their choice, and come back to your website. Orders are confirmed automatically once Papi has confirmed the payment.

  • eCommerce checkout, customer portal invoices, online quotations and payment links
  • "Pay with Papi" button on invoices and sales orders: a back-office operator can collect a payment on behalf of the customer
  • MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money and card (BRED), chosen on the Papi page
  • Signed server-to-server notifications (HMAC-SHA256), replay protection
  • Status always re-checked with the Papi API: a customer return is never trusted as a proof of payment
  • Idempotent processing: repeated notifications never confirm an order twice
  • Automatic catch-up of lost notifications and expired payment links
  • Papi reference, status, instrument, failure reason and error code visible on each transaction
  • "Test the Connection" and "Check Papi Status" buttons for diagnosis
  • Secrets restricted to administrators and never written to the logs

Requirements

  • Odoo 17.0, 18.0, 19.0 or 20.0 (one package per version). Tested on Community; Enterprise not tested
  • Odoo.sh or on-premise hosting. Odoo Online (SaaS) is not supported: it does not allow third-party modules.
  • A verified Papi shop with its API key and notification signing secret
  • Currency MGA; minimum amount 300 MGA
  • Odoo reachable over public HTTPS to receive the Papi notifications
Papi has no sandbox. In Odoo "Test" mode, Mobile Money payments are real. Use the Test Mode of your Papi shop with the test card to test without moving money.
